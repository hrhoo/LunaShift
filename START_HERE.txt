LUNASHIFT

OFFLINE (simplest)
  Double-click LunaShift_Open.html.
  Select Planner. Click Find an alternative to compare the included mission.

OPTIONAL ONLINE CROSS-CHECK
  Run Start_Windows.bat / Start_Mac.command / start_linux.sh,
  or run: python3 run.py
  Open the localhost address printed in the terminal.
  Inside Planner, select Data, online check & imports.
  Internet access is needed only for the requested online check.

No internet connection is required for the packaged mission calculations.
Online checking does not automatically add locations, dates or terrain.

QUICK DEMONSTRATION
  Planner -> Find an alternative -> Explore all tested sites & dates.
  Click cases in the matrix; inspect the location-only and date-only comparisons.
  Focus B and click Data transmission to inspect a powered radio interval.
  Read the Battery, Harvested energy and Link margin plots.

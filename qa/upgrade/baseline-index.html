Optional online Horizons cross-check

When LunaShift is opened through the included local launcher, it can request an independent airless Sun/Earth azimuth and elevation check from JPL Horizons. The online check does not silently replace the mission calculation.

Open through the launcher to enable this optional check.

Workspace and site data

Imported sites require numerical terrain or a numerical horizon. Images are not treated as elevation data.

Sources, coverage and limitations

Prepared terrain is used for the loaded study sites. The local 3D mesh explains the nearby surface; the analytical horizon may include terrain beyond the displayed patch.

Sun and Earth directions use the packaged ephemeris. Dates outside its coverage are rejected instead of replaced with invented data.
